Model → data → result.

Every step runs the real pipeline in this folder: downloads ten leagues, retrains the ensemble, pulls live odds and scores the model against the market.

01

Download data

Ten leagues of results from football-data.co.uk, then fixtures from football-data.org and the Europa League from Wikipedia.

8 steps · ~6 min · retrain after
02

Train models

Six classifiers per league with hyper-parameter search and a soft voting ensemble — O/U 2.5, 1X2, BTTS, O/U 1.5 and O/U 3.5.

3 steps · ~35 min
03

Predict & compare

Scores every upcoming fixture, fetches live 1X2 / O-U 2.5 / BTTS prices and turns the gap into an edge per market.

6 steps · ~3 min
04

Grade results

Every run is archived. This pulls the finished scores and scores each pick — 1X2, goals, BTTS and every value bet.

1 step · ~30 s
